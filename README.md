# I2EA AI Governance Ecosystem

**Institution-to-Execution Architecture (I2EA)**

A proposed shared infrastructure for interoperable AI governance, connecting institutions, policies, technologies, execution controls, and assurance.

## Overview

I2EA explores how human-defined governance requirements can be connected to machine-readable policies, operational decisions, execution controls, evidence, monitoring, and assurance.

The architecture is designed to support interoperability across organizations, jurisdictions, and technical systems without requiring all participants to adopt identical internal processes.

I2EA is presented through three complementary architecture maps:

- **Information Loop Map:** The information structures, processes, and relationships connecting facts, policies, evaluations, decisions, execution, and outcomes.
- **Governance Ecosystem Map:** The institutional and organizational arrangements supporting governance, accountability, coordination, and oversight.
- **Technical Architecture Map:** The technical components and operational mechanisms connecting governance requirements to controlled execution, evidence, and monitoring.

Together, these maps illustrate how institutional governance, information management, and technical implementation can operate as an interconnected architecture.

## Project Sakura

**Project Sakura** is a fictional organizational scenario developed to illustrate how I2EA governance concepts may operate in practical business situations.

It explores policy changes, authorization, information access, human oversight, execution controls, and governance-related incidents through a series of illustrated episodes.

The project provides an accessible introduction to the operational challenges addressed by I2EA.

**[Explore Project Sakura](projects/sakura/README.md)**

## Project Status

I2EA is an independent research initiative presenting a proposed reference architecture for AI governance.

The published materials are intended to support discussion, evaluation, technical exploration, and further development.

The architecture maps, research papers, and illustrative scenarios may be revised as the research progresses.
